## Research Use

These facts are context filters, not entry signals. The backtest should compare normal sessions against pre-event, event-day, and post-event windows while preserving chronological train/test separation. Administrative announcements and ESOP allotments should be excluded from the material-event set.

The FY26 combination of strong revenue/order visibility and weaker cash conversion should be treated as a two-sided regime: constructive for trend continuation when price and volume confirm, but vulnerable to sharp de-rating moves when execution, receivables, or margins disappoint.
